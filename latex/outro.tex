\section*{Statement on use of AI tools and individual contributions}


\subsection*{Use of AI tools}
In line with what is stated in the introduction, the authors declare that
an AI tool, specifically Claude by Anthropic, was used to support the
development of this work.\\ In particular, it was used to:
\begin{itemize}
    \item draft the Python scripts, used to post-process the simulation data
    and to automate the search for the optimal configuration of decoupling capacitors;
    \item help developing portions of the MATLAB scripts;
    \item revise parts of this report and handle Latex formatting.
\end{itemize}
All LTspice simulations were run by the authors. The results of the
scripts were cross-checked against direct LTspice simulations, and the
whole content of the report was reviewed by the authors, who take full
responsibility for it.
